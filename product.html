Description+

Repair the Biology. Reverse the Damage. Reclaim Your Skin.

A physician-formulated regenerative serum that visibly lifts, densifies, and restores skin not by masking the signs of aging, but by activating the biological pathways responsible for structural renewal.

Long before "longevity skincare" became a trend, Clark's Botanicals was studying the cellular resilience mechanisms of the Amalfi Coast, one of the world's most biodiverse and protected coastal ecosystems, and translating them into measurable pathways for human skin. In this uniquely protected environment, botanicals and marine organisms thrive under constant environmental stress. DNA-42 captures the science behind that survival.

DNA-42 Clinicalift™ Serum is the first expression of Amalfi Born Biotechnology™, and the first topical serum to combine plant-derived exosome delivery technology with a Vegan PDRN regenerative complex. Together, they ensure that repair signals reach the dermal cells where visible renewal truly begins. Polydeoxyribonucleotide (PDRN) has long been studied in regenerative dermatology historically sourced from animal tissue.

DNA-42 advances this science entirely: our plant-derived Vegan PDRN complex, drawn from rice and barley, is clinically shown to outperform legacy animal-derived formulations in collagen synthesis, fibroblast vitality, and epidermal renewal. This is not an ethical compromise. It is a clinically superior evolution. The result is a serum that works with your skin's biology not against it. Skin appears smoother, clearer, and more luminous not because something has been erased, but because the skin's own structural vitality has been rebuilt.

Your skin's been keeping score. DNA-42 is here to settle it.

How to Use+

Apply 3-5 drops to cleansed face AM and/or PM. Press gently into skin using fingertips, working outward from the center of the face. Allow to absorb fully before layering moisturizer or SPF.

For optimal results, use consistently through a complete 42-day renewal cycle. Do not interrupt the cycle, visible structural change requires the full biological timeline.

Pro Tip: Unlike retinol or strong acid treatments, DNA-42 works with your skin's biology rather than against it. There is no downtime, no cycling, and no irritation phase. You can layer it with your existing routine from day one. Sensitive skin approved.

Amalfi Born Biotechnology™

The Amalfi Coast is not just one of the world’s most beautiful coastlines. It is one of its most demanding. Shaped by the Campanian volcanic arc, its soils are saturated with ancient mineral deposits: sulfur, potassium, magnesium, and trace elements forged under intense geological pressure.

The species that thrive anyway are classified as extremophiles: organisms that have evolved not just to tolerate extreme conditions, but to flourish under them. Amalfi Born Biotechnology™ is our proprietary research platform for translating those cellular resilience mechanisms into measurable pathways for human skin.
